Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Vlad Statnick ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through this website, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

Information you give us. When you use our contact form, call, or email us, we may collect your name, email address, phone number, the type of service you're interested in, and any details you include in your message.

Information collected automatically. Like most websites, our hosting provider may log basic technical data such as your IP address, browser type, pages visited, and the date and time of your visit.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your inquiries and provide real estate services you request
  • To send you listings, market updates, or follow-ups related to your inquiry
  • To operate, maintain, and improve our website
  • To comply with legal and regulatory obligations

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share it only:

  • With service providers who help us run our business (e.g., website hosting, email, CRM), under confidentiality obligations
  • With other parties to a real estate transaction (e.g., lenders, title companies, inspectors) when you ask us to
  • When required by law, or to protect our rights or the safety of others

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All of the categories above exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

4. Text Messaging (SMS)

If you choose to opt in to text messages on our contact form, we collect your mobile number, the type of messages you agreed to (customer care and appointment messages, marketing messages, or both), and the date, time, and page where you gave consent. We use this only to send the messages you asked for and to keep a record of your consent.

  • Opting in is optional and is not a condition of any purchase or service.
  • Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.
  • Reply STOP to any message to opt out, or HELP for help.
  • Your mobile number and SMS consent are never sold, rented, or shared with third parties or affiliates for their marketing purposes.

See our SMS Terms & Conditions for full program details.

5. Cookies

This website currently uses only the cookies that are strictly necessary for it to function. If we add analytics or advertising tools in the future, we will update this policy and, where required, ask for your consent.

6. Data Retention

We keep your information only as long as needed to respond to you, provide our services, and meet legal, accounting, or licensing requirements.

7. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your information. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, but we work to protect your data.

8. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information, or to opt out of marketing communications. To make a request, contact us using the details below. You can unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time using the link in the email.

9. Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

10. Third-Party Links

Our website may link to other sites (such as social media or listing services). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it was last revised.
